The Founder

Built from service,
driven by passion.

The founder of ColdOnes Event Solutions started the company through a simple favor for a close friend and fellow veteran. When his buddy was preparing to retire, an impromptu retirement party came together quickly, and he reached out looking for someone to handle the music and entertainment for the event. Wanting to make sure the celebration was memorable, he stepped in, handled the music, elevated the atmosphere, and helped bring the event to life.

Built on values of reliability, professionalism, and service, ColdOnes Event Solutions is committed to evolving into a one-stop event experience that clients can trust from start to finish.
